About Critter Logic

Critter Logic is an independent educational channel. Each short starts with one intriguing question, follows the clues, and gives the scientific answer at the end. Episodes are illustrated with original animations and narrated in English.

How we publish

Videos are rendered by our own pipeline and published to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook by Critter Logic Publisher, our in-house tool. On TikTok it uses Login Kit to connect our creator account and the Content Posting API to upload each finished short with its caption and privacy settings. The tool is only used for our own accounts.

Privacy Policy – Critter Logic Publisher

Last updated: 2026-10-26.

Critter Logic Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Critter Logic channel to publish its own videos to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok through their official APIs.

Data we access

Only the owner's own account data needed to upload and publish videos (account identifier, display name, upload status).

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings; local tokens are then deleted.
